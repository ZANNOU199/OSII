## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the authentic, generous spirit of modern West African street gastronomy elevated into a contemporary urban dining institution. Originating in Godomey, Benin, the aesthetic bridges neighborhood warmth with polished culinary professionalism. It celebrates the artisanal fire of freshly grilled chawarmas and the chilled, creamy comfort of handcrafted dèguè (mil and couscous).

The visual language draws inspiration from contemporary Afro-modernist editorial style combined with tactile hospitality UI:
- **Atmosphere:** Sun-drenched, aromatic, convivial, and grounded. It shuns sterile tech tropes and cold corporate blues in favor of earthy clays, amber spice tones, and fermented millet neutrals.
- **Audience:** Urban Beninese youth, families, diaspora returnees, and food lovers seeking trusted local flavors served with flawless modern convenience and pristine hygiene standards.
- **Emotional Response:** Immediate appetite appeal, genuine Beninese hospitality (*bienvenue chaleureuse*), and unshakeable pride in local craftsmanship.
- **Copy Persona:** Warm, proud, respectful, and direct French phrasing reflecting local gastronomy rituals (*"Fraîchement braisé"*, *"Douceur glacée"*, *"Piment doux ou piquant ?"*).

## Colors

The palette is rooted in West African culinary traditions: wood smoke, dried chili peppers, palm oil luster, golden sun-ripened grains, and freshly plucked aromatic leaves.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#C83818` - Piment Rouge / Terre Cuite):** The vibrant red of freshly ground pepper and hot coals. Used for primary calls-to-action, cart confirmations, order alerts, and signature brand accents.
- **Secondary (`#E07A1F` - Ocre Solaire / Épice Dorée):** The deep golden amber of roasting marinades and warm sunset street lamps. Applied to specialty badges, highlight ribbons, and rewards programs.
- **Tertiary (`#15803D` - Feuille Fraîche):** A crisp, natural botanical green designating organic ingredients, order completion states, vegetarian options, and freshness guarantees.
- **Neutral Base (`#1C1917` - Charbon Chaud):** A warm off-black rich in umami depth. Provides sharp, WCAG AAA-compliant readability without the harshness of pure `#000000`.
- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base Background: `#FCF9F5` (Crème Mil & Lait - warm sand).
  - Elevated Card Surface: `#FFFFFF` (Pur & Net).
  - Tonal Surface / Input Fill: `#F4EFE6` (Farine Dorée).
  - Subtle Dividing Border: `#E8DFD1` (Tissage Naturel).

All text combinations against the `#FCF9F5` and `#FFFFFF` canvases exceed WCAG AA contrast standards (minimum 4.5:1 for body copy; 7:1+ for `#1C1917`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the bold, sculptural confidence of **Syne** with the clarity, welcoming curve, and optical precision of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Display & Headings (Syne):** Brings artisanal presence, bold pride, and an unmistakable culinary identity. Weights 700 and 800 convey the energy of live flame rotisseries and vibrant Cotonou-Godomey boulevard storefronts.
- **Body & Functional UI (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Balances humanist warmth with exceptional mobile readability. Its open apertures ensure prices in FCFA, ingredient lists, and spice levels remain effortless to scan in bright outdoor sunlight.
- **Numerical Formatting:** Menu prices must always be rendered with tabular figures (`tnum`) in Plus Jakarta Sans Bold (e.g., `2 500 FCFA`) to ensure aligned ticket totals and intuitive bill review.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is driven by an 8pt baseline grid tailored for fast mobile ordering, street-side pickups, and kitchen ticket clarity.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Grid System
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4-column fluid layout. Canvas margin: `1rem` (16px). Inter-column gutter: `1rem` (16px). Bottom sticky navigation bar reserved for order actions with safe-area padding (`env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`).
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 8-column layout. Canvas margin: `2rem` (32px). Column gutter: `1.25rem` (20px). Two-column card layouts for Chawarmas, Plats, and Dèguè desserts.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout capped at a maximum width of `1280px` centered. Canvas margin: `3rem` (48px). Column gutter: `1.5rem` (24px). Splits into a 8-column menu gallery and a 4-column live order/cart panel.

### Touch Targets
All interactive targets (increment buttons, sauce selectors, category filter tabs) adhere to a minimum physical bounding box of `48px × 48px` to guarantee zero miss-clicks during rapid on-the-go ordering.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm ambient lighting and soft, natural layering rather than stark synthetic elevation.

- **Surface Tiering:**
  - `Level 0 (Canvas):` Warm Sand (`#FCF9F5`).
  - `Level 1 (Default Containers & Product Cards):` Crisp Chalk White (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a gentle ochre-tinted boundary `1px solid #E8DFD1`.
  - `Level 2 (Active Sheets, Dropdowns & Modals):` Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a warm, diffuse ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(200, 56, 24, 0.04)`.
  - `Level 3 (Floating Order Bar & Sticky Cart Summary):` Elevated above scroll planes with an assertive warm underglow: `box-shadow: 0 20px 30px -10px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.12), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(200, 56, 24, 0.08)`.
- **Atmospheric Highlights:** Selected food categories utilize an inner micro-border (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 2px #C83818`) instead of heavy dropshadows, keeping the interface crisp, hygienic, and modern.

## Shapes

The interface embraces organic, generous curves reflecting the shape of rolled Lebanese-Beninese flatbreads, round clay fermentation pots, and natural calabash bowls.

- **Base Radius (`roundedness: 2` - 0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to form inputs, inline counters, and auxiliary alerts.
- **Card & Sheet Radius (`rounded-lg` - 1rem / 16px):** Standard for product showcases, menu items, order cards, and modal dialogs. Ensures a friendly, non-industrial visual tactile quality.
- **Pill Radius (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Strictly applied to interactive pills, primary action buttons, status chips, spice meters, and price badges. Evokes handheld street-food snackability and smooth ergonomics.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary ("Commander", "Valider le Panier"):** Pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`), background `#C83818`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `Plus Jakarta Sans 700`, height `52px`, horizontal padding `24px`. Hover state shifts to `#A92E13`; active state applies micro-scaling (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary ("Ajouter au panier", "Personnaliser"):** White background, border `1.5px solid #E07A1F`, text `#E07A1F`, pill-shaped, height `44px`. Hover background transitions to `#FEF7EE`.
- **Icon / Quantity Steppers (`-` / `+`):** 44px circular pills with `#F4EFE6` fill and `#1C1917` iconography, expanding to `#C83818` on maximum selection.

### 2. Badges & Chips
- **Category Filter Pills:** Horizontal scrolling pills. Active filter: background `#1C1917`, text `#FFFFFF`. Inactive filter: background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E8DFD1`, text `#1C1917`.
- **Specialty Culinary Tags:**
  - *Chawarma Signature:* `#C83818` background, `#FFFFFF` text, bold uppercase label.
  - *Dèguè Artisanal:* `#E07A1F` soft background tint (`#FEF7EE`), border `1px solid #E07A1F`, text `#A85207`.
  - *Piment / Épicé:* Pill with fire icon, red background `#FDE8E4`, text `#C83818`.
  - *Fait Maison / Frais:* Leaf green background `#EAF6ED`, text `#15803D`.

### 3. Food Cards (Chawarma & Dèguè)
- Built on `#FFFFFF` with `16px` border-radius and `1px solid #E8DFD1` outline.
- **Layout:** High-definition photography with an aspect ratio of 16:10, topped with a top-left floating pill badge ("Populaire à Godomey") and top-right price tag formatted in `FCFA`.
- Content stack: Syne Bold title, 2-line appetizing French description in `body-sm`, and an immediate "Ajouter" action button pinned to the lower right.

### 4. Selection Controls (Sauces, Garnitures, Taille)
- **Radio & Checkbox Buttons:** Custom circular/square elements with `22px` dimension. Active selection fills with `#C83818` and a crisp `#FFFFFF` checkmark or dot.
- Container wraps each modifier option in a clickable card row (minimum height `56px`) with clear price surcharges (`+ 500 FCFA`) aligned to the right.

### 5. Form & Input Fields
- Background `#F4EFE6`, border `1px solid #E8DFD1`, height `52px`, rounded `12px`.
- Placeholder text in `#78716C`. Focus state applies a crisp `#C83818` border and `0 0 0 3px rgba(200, 56, 24, 0.15)` focus ring.
- Labels sit above inputs in `label-md` uppercase typography with `font-weight: 700`.

### 6. Order Tracking & Status Bar
- Visual multi-step progress bar styled with warm terracotta milestones:
  1. *Commande reçue*
  2. *En préparation à Godomey*
  3. *Chawarma au feu / Dèguè frais prêt*
  4. *En route / Prêt pour retrait*
- Active status illuminated by pulsing `#E07A1F` ring, with completed steps marked in vibrant tertiary `#15803D`.
